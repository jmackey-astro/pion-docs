# PION example: NEMO microphysics with two ionising stars on the symmetry
# axis of a 2D cylindrical grid (needs PION built with -DPION_NEMO=ON).
# The stars are 3 pc apart in gas with n_H ~ 1100 per cm^3.  Their HII
# regions start separate (Stromgren radii 1.0 and 0.67 pc) and expand as
# D-type ionisation fronts.  Their shells meet at about 0.07 Myr, and the
# HII regions join along the axis by 0.2 Myr.
# The microphysics timestep limit (EP_MP_timestep_limit 1) is needed while
# the gas is first heated; after 0.07 Myr the run can be restarted with
# limit_timestep=0 on the command line, to go much faster.
# Elements H, He and O, with all ionisation stages of O.
# Grid: 128 x 64 cells (8 x 4 pc), 0.0625 pc per cell.
